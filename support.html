Support

We're here to help.

Kiln is a private, on-device AI meeting assistant for Mac and iPhone/iPad. Record or import a meeting and turn it into transcripts, summaries, action items, Jira stories, and more — without any of it leaving your device.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Kiln has no login and no subscription.

Why can't I generate reports on my device?

Report generation needs Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 16 or 17, or an M1 or later Mac/iPad). Recording and transcription still work on older supported devices.

Where are my recordings stored?

Only on your device, in the app's private sandbox. Deleting a meeting deletes its audio.

What languages are supported?

English in this release.

How do I export a report?

Open any report and use Share to export as Markdown or a paginated PDF, copy it, or save the original audio recording.
