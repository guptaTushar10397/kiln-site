Support

We're here to help. This page describes Kiln 4.1.

Kiln is a private AI meeting assistant for Mac, iPhone and iPad. Record or import a meeting and turn it into transcripts, summaries, action items, Jira stories and more, all on your device. Kiln works in nine languages.

Getting started

Do I need an account?

No. Kiln has no sign-in and no subscription.

Where do I find Settings?

On iPhone, tap the button at the top left of your library. On iPad and Mac, it's at the bottom of the sidebar.

How do I share a report?

Open the report and use Copy to paste it anywhere, or Share PDF to send or save it as a PDF. To share the original recording, open the meeting, or touch and hold it in your library (Control-click on Mac).

Where are my recordings stored?

On your device, inside Kiln. Deleting a meeting deletes its recording, transcript, speaker names and every report made from it, and Kiln can't undo it, though a backup made earlier still has the meeting until that backup is replaced. Settings → Storage → Delete All Meetings removes them all at once.

Is the AI always accurate?

No AI is perfect. Transcripts, speaker names and reports can contain mistakes, so please check important details before you share them or make decisions.

Languages

What languages are supported?

Kiln transcribes nine languages: English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Português (Brazil), 中文 (Simplified Mandarin), 日本語 and 한국어. The app itself is available in the same nine. Hindi and mixed Hindi-English aren't supported.

How do I change the app's language?

Go to Settings → App Language and pick one. Kiln switches straight away, with no restart. On iPhone and iPad, it's the same setting as Kiln's Language option in the Settings app.

How do I change the transcription language?

Settings → Transcription → Language sets the language for new meetings. You can also change it when you name a new recording or import, just before Kiln transcribes it, and that choice becomes your new default. This is separate from App Language: one is the language Kiln speaks to you in, the other is the language you speak in.

What language will my reports be in?

The language the meeting was transcribed in. Set a meeting to German and you get a German summary, title and speaker-name suggestions. Kiln doesn't detect the language on its own, so make sure the right one is picked. The headings and buttons around a report follow your App Language, and copied or PDF reports use English section headings.

Do languages need to be downloaded?

Sometimes. If your device doesn't already have a language, it downloads it from Apple the first time you use it, and Kiln shows the progress while it does. Your device manages these downloads, so Kiln can't delete them; the system frees the space when it needs to.

Why is a permission request in a different language from Kiln?

Requests like microphone access come from your device, not Kiln, so they follow your device's language. Set Kiln to Spanish on an English iPhone and the microphone request still appears in English. It isn't a missing translation.

Why is some text still in English after I switch languages?

Some error messages, such as when a report can't be made, are still in English. Everything else follows your App Language. If a translation reads wrong, please tell us.

Your device

Why can't I make reports on my device?

Reports use Apple Intelligence, so your device needs to support it and have it turned on. In Kiln, Settings → Apple Intelligence tells you whether it's ready, and apple.com/apple-intelligence lists the requirements. Kiln never sends your meeting to the cloud instead. Titles and speaker-name suggestions need Apple Intelligence too; recording, importing, transcription and telling speakers apart don't.

Kiln says transcription isn't available on this device. What does that mean?

Some devices can't transcribe on the device itself. It depends on the device, so changing the language or updating Kiln won't help. Kiln tells you plainly when this is the case.

Can I still record on a device that can't transcribe?

Not at the moment. Kiln keeps a recording only when it can transcribe it, so on these devices a new recording can't be saved. We know this isn't ideal; if it affects you, please tell us.

What's different between the Mac app and the iPhone and iPad app?

Nearly everything is the same, calendar reminders included. Only on iPhone and iPad: your recording shows on the Lock Screen (and in the Dynamic Island on iPhone), and Kiln tells you when an update is out; on a Mac, the App Store does that. Only on Mac: ⌘N and ⌘I start a recording or import audio from anywhere in the app.

Privacy and data

Does Kiln connect to the internet?

Kiln for Mac never does. On iPhone and iPad, Kiln connects for one thing only: asking the App Store whether a newer version is out. It sends nothing about you or your meetings, and it goes only to Apple. Like any internet connection, it lets Apple see your device's internet address (its IP address), which can show roughly where you are. Updates are never forced. Language downloads come from Apple, through your device.

What happens on my device?

Everything that touches your meetings: transcription, telling speakers apart, titles, speaker-name suggestions and reports. None of it uses a server, ours or anyone else's.

How do calendar reminders work?

They're off until you turn them on. Kiln offers them when you set up the app, or go to Settings → Permissions → Calendar Reminders. One tap asks for calendar and notification access, and Kiln then reminds you shortly before each meeting. Tap the reminder and Kiln opens ready to record, with the meeting already named. Kiln only reads your calendar, on your device, and never changes or sends your events. If you said no to a permission, the same screen has an Open Settings button to change that. Works on Mac, iPhone and iPad.

If I delete Kiln, is my data gone?

On iPhone and iPad, yes: deleting Kiln removes everything it stored. A backup made earlier keeps its copy until it's replaced, and so do files you shared or saved elsewhere. On a Mac, no. Moving Kiln to the Trash leaves its data behind. To remove it, also delete the folder ~/Library/Containers/com.tushargupta.kiln.

Getting help

How do I report a problem or send feedback?

In Kiln, go to Settings → Help & Feedback → Report an Issue. Kiln opens an email in your mail app with your device details and a diagnostic log attached; turn either off if you'd rather not include them. Nothing is sent until you send it. If no mail account is set up, a plain draft opens instead, without the attachments. Or email support.kiln@gmail.com.

What's in the diagnostic log?

A short record of what the app did, such as which step ran, how many speakers it found and how long the transcript was, plus your app version and device model. It never includes your recordings, transcripts, meeting titles, speaker names or reports. Preview Diagnostic Log in the app shows the log exactly as it would be sent. Apple's crash reports are attached too, and Kiln adds nothing of yours to them.
