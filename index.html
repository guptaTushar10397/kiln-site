Kiln

Meetings, fired into work.

A 100% on-device AI meeting assistant for Mac and iPhone. Record or import a meeting and turn it into transcripts, summaries, action items, and Jira stories — without a byte ever leaving your device.

100% on-device No account Free

What Kiln does

🎙️

Capture

Record with a live waveform, or import MP3, M4A, or WAV. Kiln can even name the meeting from its own words.

💬

Understand

A speaker-separated transcript you can search, edit, and play back — with on-device name suggestions backed by the transcript.

📋

Generate

Eight report types — summary, action items, Jira stories, decisions, minutes, email, release notes — all on-device.

Private by construction

No cloud, no account, no subscription, no analytics. Your recordings, transcripts, and reports stay in the app's private sandbox on your device. Delete a meeting and its audio is gone with it.
