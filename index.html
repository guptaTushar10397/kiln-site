Privacy

Private by design.
Not by promise.

Kiln has no server of its own. Your meetings are processed on your device, and Kiln never sends them anywhere.

Everything runs on your device.

Transcription, speaker separation, titles and reports all run locally, using Apple’s on-device frameworks plus speaker-separation models that ship inside the app.

The Mac app can’t go online.

It ships without permission to open a network connection, so Kiln itself can’t connect to anything, not even to check for updates.

No account. No analytics.

Nothing to sign up for, and no tracking, advertising or analytics code inside.

Delete means delete.

Delete a meeting and Kiln removes its audio, transcript, speaker records and every report made from it. Copies in backups you’ve already made stay there until those backups are replaced.

Never miss a meeting note.

Kiln reads your calendar on your device and reminds you just before a meeting starts. It never edits your events, and Kiln never sends them anywhere.

Tap the reminder and Kiln opens ready to record, with the meeting already named.

Mac, iPhone and iPad

Made for each device
you meet on.

A native app for Mac, iPhone and iPad, with the touches each one deserves.

  • On a Mac, ⌘N starts a recording and ⌘I imports audio, from anywhere in the app.
  • On iPhone, a Live Activity keeps the recording on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with a Stop button.
  • Meeting reminders work on all three.
